--- SIM-05c  is the bounce burst suppressed? ---
  The question is NOT whether the node wobbles during the burst - it
  always does, and early chatter pulses may be too short to assert at
  all. A spurious edge means: once the GPIO has ALREADY read a valid
  low (node crossed VIL = 0.8 V), does the node climb back above
  VIH = 2.0 V before the closure genuinely ends at 20 ms?
  So: find the first VIL crossing, then take the peak from there to
  19.9 ms. Peak above VIH = a false release the decoder would count.

   Cf        t_first_assert(ms)  peak V after it   result
   1e-9    10.001    3.29967    FAIL - false release, extra edges
   10e-9    10.0138    2.96197    FAIL - false release, extra edges
   100e-9    11.5003    1.30683    PASS - bounce suppressed
   220e-9    11.8684    0.797069    PASS - bounce suppressed
   1e-6    12.8654    0.798565    PASS - bounce suppressed
